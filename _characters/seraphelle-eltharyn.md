## Appearance

Silver hair worn intricately braided.
Cold blue eyes.
Regal white-and-silver clothing.
Always appears perfectly composed.

## Personality

Brilliant and manipulative.
Emotionally restrained.
Believes control is necessary for survival.
Genuinely fears societal collapse.
Carries a resentment toward Aife she has never let herself fully examine, the quiet, corrosive feeling of having always been second in a way that had nothing to do with merit.

## Origin and Complicity

Seraphelle did not orchestrate Aife's exile, Velmourn did, working through years of patient influence over the court's dreams and memory. But Seraphelle knew, at the time, that something about the scandal used against her younger sister didn't sit right, and she chose not to look closer.

In the aftermath she felt real guilt, and told herself for a while she'd eventually bring Aife back. She never did. As the years passed and the throne settled comfortably onto her, the guilt curdled into a quiet, permanent relief that the decision had already been made for her, and a resentment she's never spoken aloud, that Aife, younger and never meant to inherit anything, had still somehow always been the one people found more compelling.

## Abilities

**Information Dominion:** Extensive surveillance systems, psychological manipulation, political control, and advanced memory-altering technology.

## Character Arc

Seraphelle represents civilization sacrificing freedom in exchange for stability, and a person sacrificing a sibling in exchange for a version of herself she prefers. Her downfall shows how fear corrodes even noble intentions, how convenient guilt can calcify into justification, and how little any of it ultimately had to do with the actual, much older danger underneath everything.

Stripped of power but spared exile by Aife, a mercy she initially mistakes for pity, she spends the story's final stretch slowly earning her way back into something resembling trust, culminating in genuine, hands-on usefulness when it matters most.
