- **Magic/Power:** A rare, high concentration of Requezilla, the life-force behind Aldrean's magic

## Character Bio

Denji grew up in Bright Hill as the estranged son of its ruling Matrix. More soldier than family, disciplined and used to being alone. He's spent years being praised for surviving things he shouldn't, though the people around him have started to notice how little those wounds seem to cost him. Guarded but fiercely loyal, he'd rather take the damage of a bad decision himself than let someone else pay for it.
