## Appearance

Long white hair.
Thin build.
Pale skin.
Violet-gray eyes.
Elegant layered robes covered in ancient symbols, worn now more like a scholar's coat than the ceremonial dress they were originally cut from.
Usually carries old records or mechanical relics.
Carries herself with a straight-backed formality that reads oddly in a prison cell, something a few of the older guards seem to defer to without quite explaining why.

## Personality

Calm and analytical.
Emotionally distant at first.
Intensely curious.
Quietly compassionate.
Fascinated by forbidden knowledge.
Fears how much history has already been forgotten, and, privately, how much of her own life has been erased along with it.

Aife is not withholding out of coyness. She has genuinely lost people the last time she let herself be known for who she was, and has decided, consciously, that being useful and unremarkable is safer than being seen.

## Origin

Aife was born the younger sister to Seraphelle, both daughters of Eodwen's ruling house. Velmourn, one of the Dread Five, spent decades quietly embedded in Eodwen's court, shaping succession politics through influence over dreams and memory. Aife, more outspoken and less controllable than her sister, was maneuvered out of the line of succession through a fabricated scandal she still cannot fully untangle from what actually happened and what was planted in her own memory. She was formally banished from Eodwen in disgrace.

She spent years afterward wandering the ruins and archives of Aldrean alone before being captured by Hold forces while investigating ruins too close to Chimera territory, and imprisoned in the same cell where Denji later wakes up.

None of this is known to Denji, Aella, or anyone else in the group early on. She doesn't lie about it, exactly. She simply never brings it up.

## Abilities

**Ancient Knowledge:** Expert in lost civilizations, understands relic systems, studies reality fractures, can interpret ancient archives, and is skilled in barrier and seal magic, all rooted in a royal education most of the group has no reason to suspect she received. Her knowledge should read as genuine expertise, not omniscience. She theorizes, infers, and occasionally guesses wrong.

**The Engineer:** The soul who spearheaded the first, failed attempt to build the Ark generations ago has been reincarnated since, most recently as Aife. This gives her a personal, aching stake in the Ark succeeding this time that goes beyond scholarly curiosity, something she doesn't consciously remember but feels as an inexplicable, lifelong pull toward the project.

## Character Arc

Aife begins detached from the emotional conflicts around her, partly scholarly temperament and partly a defense mechanism built after losing her home once already. Over time she becomes emotionally invested in the group and in preserving humanity itself. Her arc eventually forces her to confront Eodwen again, and Seraphelle, and the version of her own story the rest of the world believes.

After Eodwen falls, the throne passes to Aife by right of blood. She chooses not to exile Seraphelle in return, refusing to repeat the pattern that shaped her own life.

## Relationships

**Aella** — Studies her as a Clock-Bearer while slowly becoming protective of her.

**Seraphelle** — Older sister and Eodwen's ruler at the story's start. Aife's exile is the quiet reason the throne passed to Seraphelle at all. The two never fully reconcile in the traditional sense, but eventually work the same side of the same catastrophe.

**Denji** — Respects his humanity despite his terrifying potential, and, unspoken, recognizes something of her own situation in a person who did not choose the thing that now defines how everyone sees him.
