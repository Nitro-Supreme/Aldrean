## Character Bio

A scholar of lost civilizations, Aife was captured by Hold forces while investigating ruins too close to Chimera territory. She carries herself with a formality that doesn't quite fit a prison cell, and rarely talks about her life before it. Calm, curious, and careful with what she reveals about herself, she'd rather be useful than known.
