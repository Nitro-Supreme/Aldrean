## Character Bio

A respected officer and strategist within Bright Hill's command, Erembour carries himself with a stillness that reads as discipline. Those who know him longest suspect it's something closer to exhaustion. He argues unpopular positions in council and rarely seems bothered by losing the argument, though something underneath that composure suggests otherwise.
