The armory sat at the lowest habitable level of the Sungrave Citadel, in a place most soldiers had no reason to visit and fewer reasons to linger.

The stonework changed as one descended toward it. The upper fortress was built of carefully dressed granite, fitted and mortared with the precision of a city that had spent generations deciding what it wanted to look like. Deeper down, that certainty eroded.
